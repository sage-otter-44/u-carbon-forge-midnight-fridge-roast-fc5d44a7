# Midnight Fridge Roast

One line roasting a smart fridge that locks itself after midnight in the name of "wellness mode" — silent for three weeks while the milk expired, but drawing the line at a 12:01 snack.

## Entry

> My fridge locks at midnight and flashes 'wellness mode' — it let the milk expire in silence for three weeks, but my 12:01 snack is where it draws the line.
